# Lemon olive oil cake

Loose, damp, keeps for three days under a tea towel.

## Ingredients

- 200 g caster sugar
- 3 eggs
- 150 ml olive oil, the peppery kind
- Zest and juice of 2 lemons
- 200 g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Oven to 170°C. Line a 20 cm tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs until pale, about three minutes.
3. Stream in the oil, then the zest and juice.
4. Fold in the flour, baking powder and salt. Do not over-mix.
5. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes. A skewer comes out clean, the top cracks a little.

Serve after [[Shakshuka]] with crème fraîche, or on its own the next morning.
