# Shakshuka

The Tuesday one. Bread is not optional.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, a pinch of chilli
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- Feta, parsley, good bread

## Method

1. Soften the onion and pepper in olive oil for ten minutes. Slower is better.
2. Add the garlic and spices, cook for one minute more.
3. Tomatoes in, plus half a tin of water. Simmer for fifteen minutes until it thickens.
4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, lid on, four to five minutes.
5. Feta and parsley over the top. Eat from the pan.

> [!tip] Make it stretch
> Double the sauce and freeze half. It thaws into a pasta sauce with no effort.
